石晶地板作为一种新型的地面装饰材料，在浴室地板的选择中脱颖而出，成为了最佳选择。以下是详细原因：<br>
<br>
原材料组成<br>
石晶地板主要由PVC树脂粉、石粉、塑化剂、稳定剂、炭黑等组成。其中，PVC树脂粉是主要的基础材料，具有良好的柔韧性和耐腐蚀性，能够保证地板的基本性能和使用寿命。石粉则是天然大理石粉，不仅赋予了地板高密度、高纤维网状结构的坚实基层，还使地板更加环保健康，经过权威部门检测不含任何放射性物质。塑化剂和稳定剂的添加则进一步优化了地板的加工性能和稳定性，确保地板在不同环境下都能保持良好的性能。炭黑的作用主要是调节地板的颜色和增加地板的耐候性。这些原材料的精心配比，使得石晶地板具备了多种优异的性能。<br>
<br>
生产工艺<br>
石晶地板的生产工艺十分精细复杂。首先，将PVC、碳酸钙、润滑系统、增韧系统等原料按照一定的比例进行混合，然后通过塑化挤出机进行高温塑化挤出，形成密实的板材。在挤出过程中，精确控制温度、压力和挤出速度等参数，以确保板材的质量和性能。挤出后的板材经过模具和辊筒成型，自然冷却后得到初步的石晶板坯。接下来，对板坯进行表面UV处理，这一过程不仅可以增加地板的光泽度和耐磨性，还能使地板具有良好的防污和抗菌性能。最后，再进行开企口、背贴等工艺加工，方便地板的安装和使用。经过这一系列的生产工艺，石晶地板具备了优异的性能和美观的外观。

防水优势<br>
石晶地板具有卓越的防水性能，这使其成为浴室地板的理想选择。其防水原理主要体现在以下几个方面：一是石晶地板的主要成分PVC树脂粉和石粉本身具有较好的防水性能，能够有效阻挡水分的渗透；二是在生产过程中，地板经过高温塑化挤出和密实成型，形成了致密的结构，水分难以进入地板内部；三是地板的企口设计和安装方式，使得地板之间的拼接紧密，进一步防止了水分从缝隙中渗入。即使在浴室这样潮湿的环境中，长期接触水也不会导致地板变形、发霉、腐烂等问题，有效延长了地板的使用寿命，减少了因潮湿而引发的安全隐患，如滑倒等。<br>
<br>
安装注意事项<br>
在安装石晶地板时，需要注意以下几点：首先，地面必须平整、干燥、清洁，如有不平整的地方需要先进行找平处理，否则会影响地板的安装效果和使用寿命。其次，安装前要确保室内温度和湿度适宜，一般温度在15℃-30℃，湿度在40%-60%之间为宜，避免因环境因素导致地板变形。再者，安装时要注意地板的拼接，确保企口紧密连接，可使用专业的安装工具进行辅助，如橡胶锤等，轻轻敲击地板使其拼接牢固，但要注意力度，避免损坏地板。最后，在地板安装完成后，要及时清理地板表面的杂物和灰尘，并进行适当的保养，如定期打蜡等，以保持地板的美观和性能。<br>
<br>
石晶地板凭借其优质的原材料组成、先进的生产工艺、卓越的防水优势以及相对简单的安装注意事项，成为了浴室地板的最佳选择。它不仅能够为浴室提供美观、舒适的地面装饰效果，还能有效解决浴室潮湿的问题，让您的浴室更加安全、耐用、环保。<br>

As a new type of floor decoration material, stone crystal floor stands out in the choice of bathroom floor and becomes the best choice. Here are the detailed reasons:<br>
<br>
Raw material composition<br>
<br>
Stone crystal floor is mainly composed of PVC resin powder, stone powder, plasticizer, stabilizer, carbon black, etc. Among them, PVC resin powder is the main basic material, with good flexibility and corrosion resistance, which can ensure the basic performance and service life of the floor. Stone powder is natural marble powder, which not only gives the floor a solid base with high density and high fiber mesh structure, but also makes the floor more environmentally friendly and healthy. It has been tested by authoritative departments and does not contain any radioactive substances. The addition of plasticizers and stabilizers further optimizes the processing performance and stability of the floor, ensuring that the floor can maintain good performance in different environments. The main function of carbon black is to adjust the color of the floor and increase the weather resistance of the floor. The careful proportion of these raw materials makes the stone crystal floor have a variety of excellent properties.<br>
<br>
Production process<br>
<br>
The production process of stone crystal floor is very delicate and complicated. First, PVC, calcium carbonate, lubrication system, toughening system and other raw materials are mixed in a certain proportion, and then plasticized and extruded at high temperature through a plasticizing extruder to form a dense board. During the extrusion process, parameters such as temperature, pressure and extrusion speed are precisely controlled to ensure the quality and performance of the board. The extruded board is formed by a mold and roller, and the initial stone crystal slab is obtained after natural cooling. Next, the surface of the slab is treated with UV, which not only increases the gloss and wear resistance of the floor, but also makes the floor have good anti-fouling and antibacterial properties. Finally, the tongue-and-groove opening, back-sticking and other processes are carried out to facilitate the installation and use of the floor. After this series of production processes, the stone crystal floor has excellent performance and beautiful appearance.<br>
<br>
Waterproof advantages<br>
<br>
<a href="https://www.bambooindustry.com/blog/spc-flooring-perfect-choice-for-bathroom.html">SPC flooring for bathroom</a> has excellent waterproof performance, which makes it an ideal choice for bathroom floor. Its waterproof principle is mainly reflected in the following aspects: First, the main components of the stone crystal floor, PVC resin powder and stone powder, have good waterproof performance and can effectively block the penetration of water; second, during the production process, the floor is plasticized and extruded at high temperature and compactly formed to form a dense structure, which makes it difficult for water to enter the interior of the floor; third, the tongue-and-groove design and installation method of the floor make the splicing between the floors tight, further preventing moisture from penetrating from the gaps. Even in a humid environment like the bathroom, long-term contact with water will not cause deformation, mold, rot and other problems of the floor, effectively extending the service life of the floor and reducing safety hazards caused by moisture, such as slips.<br>
<br>
Installation precautions<br>
<br>
When installing the stone crystal floor, you need to pay attention to the following points: First, the ground must be flat, dry and clean. If there are any uneven places, you need to level them first, otherwise it will affect the installation effect and service life of the floor. Secondly, before installation, make sure that the indoor temperature and humidity are appropriate. Generally, the temperature is between 15℃-30℃ and the humidity is between 40%-60% to avoid deformation of the floor due to environmental factors. Furthermore, pay attention to the splicing of the floor during installation to ensure that the tongue and groove are tightly connected. You can use professional installation tools for assistance, such as rubber hammers, etc., and gently tap the floor to make it firmly spliced, but pay attention to the strength to avoid damaging the floor. Finally, after the floor is installed, clean up the debris and dust on the surface of the floor in time, and carry out appropriate maintenance, such as regular waxing, to maintain the beauty and performance of the floor.<br>
<br>
With its high-quality raw material composition, advanced production technology, excellent waterproof advantages and relatively simple installation precautions, stone crystal flooring has become the best choice for bathroom flooring. It can not only provide a beautiful and comfortable floor decoration effect for the bathroom, but also effectively solve the problem of moisture in the bathroom, making your bathroom safer, more durable and environmentally friendly.
